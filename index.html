Frequently asked questions

Everything you need before you fly.

Visa requirements depend on your passport and destination. Most Southeast Asian and South American countries offer visa-on-arrival or e-visas for stays under 30 days. We'll flag any visa steps once you submit your trip planning form.

Free cancellation up to 14 days before departure. Between 7–14 days, a 50% credit is issued toward a future trip. Inside 7 days, packages are non-refundable unless covered by travel insurance.

Pack layers over bulk — a light rain shell and a warm mid-layer beat a single heavy coat. Roll rather than fold, keep one change of clothes in your carry-on, and leave 20% of your bag empty for the trip home.
